# Writing, documents and messages checklist

- It answers what was asked, and the main point comes first.
- Every fact, number, name, date and quote is correct and traceable to a source that was actually read. Nothing is invented, including citations and links.
- Arithmetic is recomputed, totals add up, and units are stated.
- Length fits the purpose. Filler, repetition and empty openings and closings are cut.
- Tone and level fit the reader, and unfamiliar terms are explained.
- It follows any format, template, word limit or style the user gave.
- Names are spelled correctly, and every placeholder such as `[Company]` is filled.
- Links work and point where the text says they do.
- For anything that will be sent or published, nothing private or confidential is included by accident.
- Read it once as the recipient. Fix anything they would need to read twice.
